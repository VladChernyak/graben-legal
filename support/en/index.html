Frequently asked questions

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings → Data → "Export personal data". The file contains all your records together with photos. You can restore it with "Import from file" in the same section. Personal records live only on your phone, so a backup is the only way to get them back.

How do I join a squad?

Open the invite link or scan the QR code that a squad member sent you. You will need to sign in with your email address.

Route recording stops when the screen turns off

Allow the app location access "Always": iPhone Settings → GRABEN → Location. Without it iOS stops recording along with the screen.

Does the app work without internet?

Your records, routes and imported old maps work without a connection. The satellite map loads when you have internet.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → "Delete account permanently". More about data in the Privacy Policy.
